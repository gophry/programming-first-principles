Binary

  • Number Systems
    • Hindu-Arabic Numeral System - base 10
    • Sumerians - base 60
    • Maya - base 20
    • Yuki - base 8
  • Positional Notation/Radix
    • Decimal - Positional notation with radix 10
    • Binary - Positional notation with radix 2

  • Flip every bit.
  • Add 1.

Byte Ordering

  • Endianess
    • Little Endian - Most CPUs today
      • Most significant byte(MSB) goes last
    • Big Endian - TCP/IP protocols
      • Most significant byte(MSB) goes first > Timelines
  • Big Endian - IBM 360(1964)
  • Little Endian - PDP-11(1970), Intel 8008(1972), Intel 8080/8086/x86(1974-1978),

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endianness
