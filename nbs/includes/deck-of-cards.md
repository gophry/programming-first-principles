::: {.callout-note title="Deck of Cards"}
* A deck is 52 cards. Each card is one rank in one suit.
* Four suits: spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs.
* Thirteen ranks in each suit, numbered 1 through 13.
* One card is one rank and one suit, such as 7 of hearts.
* A rank above 10 is a face card.
* The deck is every rank in every suit. 4 × 13 = 52.
:::
